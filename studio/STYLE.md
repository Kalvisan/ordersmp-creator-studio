# OrderSMP Creator Studio

Use the existing OrderSMP inventory screen design for the working interface.
Near black #1A1A1A is the base, cream #F3E6C4 is the text, red #C8102E marks
primary actions, dark red #7A0A1E gives depth and gold #FFCC28 marks focus.
Order Pixel is for short titles and buttons. Use a readable system face for
forms, permissions and descriptions. Keep the SIL licence with the font.

Start with the creator's recordings and the upload action. Follow with the
selected source, clip preview, permission and review calendar. Use square
inventory frames and native controls. There are no invented statistics or
decorative progress claims. Only show progress measured from the upload.
The owner reviews the actual clip beside its words and destinations.

The trailer outro reuses its sunset footage and the canonical Order eye.
Keep it to a quiet five-second hold, one brand name, the supplied launch date
and one community destination. Do not draw fake clickable video cards into
a Short. Real YouTube end-screen elements are configured separately on
eligible videos, with space left for them in a future landscape template.
